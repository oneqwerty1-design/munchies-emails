The Pillar Engine

§1

The Loop

Pillar ad→Pillar LP (age-gated)→PDP→Popup: “what are you here for?”→
Klaviyo pillar_interest→Pillar welcome branch→Cross-pillar ladder→Repeat
One vocabulary
The doc's “how it feels” lines are the pain copy; “how we answer” lines are the promise copy. Ads tease it, LPs agitate it, emails continue it. No channel invents parallel language.
Color = pillar
Fixed accent per pillar across ad, LP skin, and email header. Subconscious continuity from feed to inbox.
Trust = the floor
Trust isn't a fifth silo — its block (dosing, onset timeline, COA, reviews) ships on every pillar LP and in every welcome flow.
Traffic law
Paid lands on age-gated trymunchies.com pillar LPs. deltamunchies.com gets sister pages for email + SEO. Same content, two skins.

§3

Segmentation

The popup — question first, email second

Step 1 (before asking for anything): “What are you here for?” — Feeling good for no reason · The off-switch · Actually sleeping · Fun without the hangover · Just looking 👀. The answer is worth as much as the email. Step 2: email + pillar-matched incentive (Sleep gets “first-night guide + 15% off Midnight Berry,” not generic 10%). Trigger: 2nd pageview or 15s + exit intent. Writes pillar_interest + pillar_selected_at to Klaviyo.

Enrichment that keeps working

Purchase
Bought Midnight Berry → pillar_interest = sleep. Purchase beats stated intent.
Clicks
2+ clicks on pillar-tagged links in 30d → update property (powered by UTM standard, §4).
Reviews
Review request asks “what'd you use it for?” (same five options) → feeds segmentation AND builds pillar-filtered review content for LPs. Compounding asset.
Re-poll
90 days later: one-question “still here for X?” email — doubles as re-engagement.

§5

Attribution + Standards

UTMs
Match live-ad convention (Shopify Customer Journey + Meta only). Pillar always in utm_campaign: {pillar}-{concept}-{date}. Email: utm_source=klaviyo&utm_medium=email&utm_campaign={pillar}-{flow}. This powers click-based enrichment.
Codes
Pillar discounts as backup attribution: FEELGOOD15 · UNWIND15 · MIDNIGHT15 · NIGHTOUT15 · FIRSTTIME15. Auto-apply: /discount/CODE?redirect=/pages/{lp}.
Ad naming
MMDD | {PILLAR} | BROAD | EXC-CA-180D · ads {PILLAR} | {FORMAT} | {HOOK}.
KPIs
LP CVR · popup pillar distribution (= demand signal for what to feed next) · flow revenue per branch · code redemptions.
Compliance floor, all pillars: no medical claims, no intoxication language in paid copy, money-math only for anti-alcohol, paid traffic to age-gated LPs only, 21+ everywhere, no copy reuse across other brands.
